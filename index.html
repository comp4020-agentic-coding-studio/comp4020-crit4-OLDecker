Pond

Drag a pad past the pond's edge, or focus it and press Delete, to remove it. Drag a palette icon onto the pond to place a new one exactly where you drop it. Click the windmill to pause or resume the melody.

🔇 No sound? iPhones mute web audio when the side switch is set to silent. Flip it off to hear the pond.
